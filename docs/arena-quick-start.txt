SILICON VALLEY SMACKDOWN — ARENA EDITION

PLAY IN THREE STEPS
1. Extract the ZIP (Windows: right-click > Extract All).
2. Open PLAY.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3. Choose ENTER THE ARENA, select your fighters and mode, and start.

No installation, Node.js, local server, account, ROM, or internet connection is needed.
PLAY.html contains the complete Arena game, including 3D models and synthesized audio.
Use a desktop/laptop browser with WebGL 2 and hardware acceleration enabled.
This is a portable browser game, not a native Windows .exe installer.

HOW TO WIN
Build your opponent's damage, then knock them beyond the arena boundaries.
Higher damage means stronger knockback. Each fighter has three stocks.
Last fighter with stocks wins. A timeout ranks stocks first, then lower damage.
A tie starts sudden death at 150% with one stock.

CONTROLS                   PLAYER 1               PLAYER 2
Move                       A / D                  Left / Right arrows
Jump / double jump         W or Space             Up arrow
Basic / air attack         V                      J or 4
Strong directional attack  Direction + V          Direction + J
Signature projectile       B                      K or 5
Rising recovery            Hold W + tap B         Hold Up + tap K
Down special               S + B                  Down + K
Shield                     N                      L or 6
Dodge roll                 N + A or D             L + Left or Right
Grab (beats shield)        M                      ; or +
Drop through / fast fall   Hold S                 Hold Down
Pause / resume             Escape
Fullscreen                 F (or your browser's fullscreen command)
Mute                       O

Jump twice, then use up + special to get back to the stage. Recovery refreshes
when you land. Steer toward a nearby ledge to pull yourself up. A shield loses
strength while held and can break. Coffee heals 22%; GPUs boost attacks for 8s.

CONTROLLERS
Standard browser-mapped controllers are supported. Press a button to activate.
The first two detected controllers control players 1 and 2. Xbox-style names:
left stick or D-pad = move; A = jump; X = attack; B = special; Y = grab;
LT/LB/RT = shield; up + B = recovery; Start = pause.
Use the keyboard/mouse to navigate menus. Physical controller hardware was not
available in the build environment; keyboard controls were browser-tested.

MODES
Quick match: fight a CPU on any arena.
Local versus: two people on the same keyboard or two controllers.
Arcade: climb the roster to the Elon final match.
Training: unlimited stocks, no timer, stationary dummy. P2 keys control the dummy.
Pause in Training for DUMMY +50% to practice high-damage launches.

All six fighters and all three arenas are genuinely rendered as 3D meshes.
Combat is played on a horizontal plane, as in a platform fighter.

PERFORMANCE / TROUBLESHOOTING
For slower integrated graphics, uncheck High quality shadows in match setup.
If the screen reports WebGL unavailable, enable browser hardware acceleration.
Click into the game to enable audio. Browser/OS autoplay rules may delay audio.
The game pauses when you switch windows, so you do not lose a stock while away.
Small/private-browser storage restrictions only affect remembering settings.
The code targets 60 simulation ticks/second; native RTX 4070 performance has not
been measured here. Artwork is original stylized procedural 3D, not AAA models.

EDITING THE GAME

See Source/README.md and Source/docs/arena-edition.md.
Node.js 22.12+ is required only for editing/building, not for playing PLAY.html.
Classic is available when running the source with ?classic=1 in the URL.
The portable PLAY.html focuses on the six original Silicon Valley characters.
It uses no Nintendo ROM, Nintendo assets, or BattleShip code.
